Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Welcome to Push Mahjong ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you play Push Mahjong.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Push Mahjong, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Facebook Login, we receive your public display name, email address, and profile avatar URL. For guest accounts, a temporary random identifier is generated locally.
  • Game Progress & Statistics: Your game level, XP, coins, total matches played, wins, and losses are stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore.
  • Device & Usage Data: Unique device identifiers, advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID), operating system version, and general network connection state for matchmaking and advertisement delivery.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

Push Mahjong integrates trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Firebase & Firestore: Authentication, real-time database, friend matchmaking, and cloud game synchronization. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. (Google AdMob Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In: Secure user authentication. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Facebook Login: Social authentication and friend matchmaking. (Meta Privacy Policy)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide real-time 1v1 multiplayer matchmaking and friend room invitations.
  • Save and synchronize your XP, level, and game progress across devices.
  • Display non-intrusive in-game advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Maintain game integrity, security, and prevent fraudulent activity.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your game data as long as your account remains active. You have full right to request complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data at any time.

You can delete your account instantly from the in-game Settings (⚙️) -> Delete Account & Data button, or submit a request on our web page: Account & Data Deletion Page or by emailing us at ekaner81@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Push Mahjong is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at:

Email: ekaner81@gmail.com
