Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Welcome to Push Mahjong ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you play Push Mahjong.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Push Mahjong, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you create an account with Email & Password or sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your display name/username, email address, and — for Google — your profile avatar URL. For guest accounts, a temporary random identifier is generated locally on your device and no personal information is collected.
  • Game Progress & Statistics: Your game level, XP, coins, total matches played, wins, and losses are stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore.
  • Device & Usage Data: Unique device identifiers, advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID), operating system version, and general network connection state for matchmaking and advertisement delivery.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

Push Mahjong integrates trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Secure user account creation (email/password) and sign-in. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase & Firestore: Real-time cloud database, friend matchmaking, and game synchronization. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. (Google AdMob Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In: Optional secure social authentication. (Google Privacy Policy)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide real-time 1v1 multiplayer matchmaking and friend room invitations.
  • Save and synchronize your XP, level, and game progress across devices.
  • Display non-intrusive in-game advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Send password reset emails when requested by you.
  • Maintain game integrity, security, and prevent fraudulent activity.

4. Data Security

All user data is stored using Google Firebase with industry-standard encryption. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are handled exclusively and securely by Firebase Authentication using strong hashing algorithms. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your game data as long as your account remains active. You have the full right to request complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data at any time.

You can delete your account instantly from the in-game Settings (⚙️) → Delete Account & Data button, or submit a request on our web page: Account & Data Deletion Page, or by emailing us at ekaner81@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Push Mahjong is designed for general audiences (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at ekaner81@gmail.com and we will promptly delete such information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us:

Email: ekaner81@gmail.com

  • Hesap ve Profil Bilgileri: E-posta ve Şifre ile hesap oluşturduğunuzda veya Google ile Giriş yaptığınızda; kullanıcı adınız/takma adınız, e-posta adresiniz ve Google hesabınız için profil fotoğrafı URL'si alınmaktadır. Kişisel verileriniz hiçbir koşulda üçüncü taraflara satılmaz veya kiralanmaz.
